**Name:** TT

**Tagline (60자 안쪽):** A shared calendar for couples, with a ping when plans change

**Topics (예시):** Productivity · Calendar · Web App

**Description:**

> One calendar for the two of you. When your partner adds, edits or deletes a plan, your phone gets a notification —
> plus a reminder before timed plans and a summary each morning at 8. Counts your days together and anniversaries.
> Runs in the browser; add it to your home screen. Free beta, no ads.

## 메이커 첫 댓글

> Hi Product Hunt — I'm the maker.
>
> [사장님 이야기 한두 줄 — 왜 만들었는지. 실제 그대로]
>
> TT does one thing first: when one of you adds, edits or deletes a plan, the other's phone hears about it.
> Your own changes never notify you.
>
> Around that:
> - "Starting soon" 30–60 minutes before timed plans, and one summary of the day at 8am, for both of you
> - Mine / yours / ours colours, and the day you met turns into 100-day milestones and anniversaries on the calendar
> - Nine languages and public holidays for nine countries
>
> What it isn't yet:
> - It's a web app (PWA). Store apps are built but not published
> - On iPhone, notifications need Add to Home Screen first (iOS 16.4+)
> - One time zone per calendar — long-distance couples in different zones will notice
>
> It's free in beta with no ads. I'd love to hear where onboarding (Google sign-in → invite link → home screen) loses you.
