TT Open source

One calendar for the two of you

Put in a plan and your partner’s phone knows. Anniversaries and “days together” count themselves.

Start free

Opens in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it works like an app.

What it does

Mine, yours, ours

Each plan belongs to one of you, or to both — shown in its own colour.

Your partner hears about it

When one of you adds, edits or deletes a plan, the other gets a notification. Your own changes never ping you.

Reminders that just arrive

A “starting soon” note 30 minutes to an hour before timed plans, and one summary of the day at 8 in the morning.

Days together, counted

Enter the day you met: 100 days, 200 days and every anniversary appear on the calendar, with a countdown to each.

Your photos on it

Add up to 30 photos and they turn up beside plans and on empty days.

Your language, your holidays

Nine languages, public holidays for nine countries, and your own time zone.

The “Us” tab

A question a day — their answer opens once you have answered too — plus a photo diary that comes back a year later, a bucket list, a date-money ledger, food and film notes with stars, and a poke to say “thinking of you”.

A map of your places

Pin the places that matter to you two. Location sharing is off until both of you turn it on, keeps only the latest spot and is deleted the moment you turn it off.

How to start

  1. Continue with Google — one tap, no password to remember.
  2. Send your partner the invite link from Settings. It works once and lasts 7 days.
  3. Add it to your home screen and turn on notifications — on iPhone, from Safari’s Share menu.

Questions

Is TT free?

Yes. TT is free and open source (MIT), with no ads and no paid features. You can run your own copy, so your calendar stays in your own database.

Do we need to download it from an app store?

No. TT runs in the browser on iPhone, Android and computers. Adding it to the home screen makes it open like an app, and that is also how notifications work on iPhone.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open it in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. On iOS 16.4 and later it can send notifications once it is on the home screen.

How do I invite my partner?

In Settings, make an invite link and send it. When your partner signs up through it, you share the same calendar. Each of you keeps your own account.

What do you collect?

Your Google account number and email address (to know it is you), and what you put in the calendar: plans, names, dates and photos. No password. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

What if one of us deletes their account?

The calendar stays with the other person. When both of you have left, the plans and photos are deleted.

Is our location shared?

Only if both of you turn location sharing on in the “Us” tab. Only the latest spot is kept, it is sent at most once a minute while that screen is open, and turning it off deletes it.
