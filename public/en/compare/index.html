When another one fits better

  • Google Calendar, if your plans already live there, or you want meeting invites, video calls and work calendars in one place.
  • TimeTree, if a whole family or group shares one calendar, or you want to comment on plans inside the app.

Questions

What is the best shared calendar app for couples?

It depends on what you need. If everything already lives in Google Calendar, share a calendar there. If you want something made for two, with your partner’s changes announced from the start and anniversaries counted for you, TT is built for exactly that.

Can couples use Google Calendar together?

Yes. Each of you needs a Google account, and one shares a calendar with the other. To hear about each other’s changes, each of you turns on email notifications for that calendar.
